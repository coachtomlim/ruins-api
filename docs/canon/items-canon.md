# Items Canon

## Quest Items

- `LOCKED`: `Prism Fragment A`
  - Initial item from Sheja.
  - Part of Prism of Makidos assembly.
- `LOCKED`: `Prism Fragment B`
  - Dropped after Musca encounter in Room 8.
  - Part of Prism of Makidos assembly.
- `LOCKED`: `Prism Fragment C`
  - Dropped after Lizardman encounter in Room 7.
  - Part of Prism of Makidos assembly.
- `LOCKED`: `Prism of Makidos`
  - Created by assembling all three fragments.
  - Unlocks progression toward the final chamber.
- `LOCKED`: `Hexagonal Glass Piece`
  - Found in Room 6 by examining mirror shards.
  - Used at the black wall / dead-end route to activate a one-way portal.
- `LOCKED`: `Sound-Deflecting Girdle`
  - Required for Room 10 survival.
  - Auto-triggers against the Banshee shriek if possessed.
- `LOCKED`: `Merlin's Tetrahedronal`
  - Final artifact in Room 10 after defeating the Banshee Doppelganger.

## Scrolls

- `LOCKED`: `Fog of Confusion`
  - Super effective against Imp.
  - Found/dropped from Room 2 low-tier monster.
- `LOCKED`: `Pulse of Calm`
  - Super effective against Musca.
  - Found/dropped from Room 4 or Room 6 low-tier monster, depending route/version.
- `LOCKED`: `Heart Beacon`
  - Super effective against Lizardman.
  - Dropped after defeating the Imp in Room 5.

## Consumables and Equipment

- `LOCKED`: `Cure All Stats Potion`
  - Found via Room 3 sarcophagus.
  - Restores stats and cures poison/debuffs.
- `LOCKED`: `Shield of the Lionheart`
  - Store item, `+2 DEF`, `400g`.
- `LOCKED`: `Minor Combat Healing Potion`
  - Store item, single-use auto-heal at low HP, `350g`.
- `LOCKED`: `Hose of Speed`
  - Store item, `+1 EVA`, `450g`.

## Readable / Lore Items

- `LOCKED`: `Adventurer's Journal`
  - Initial item.
  - Tracks quest objective, scroll clues, lore, discoveries, final status.
- `LIKELY`: `Lorebook of the First Light`
  - Associated with Room 5 hidden panel/lore.
  - Should update journal when read or discovered.

## Conflicts and Unknowns

- `CONFLICTED`: Some docs say the Girdle is a direct item drop after the Imp; post-test fixes say it requires `Examine East Wall`, `Push Panel`, then manual pickup.
- `CONFLICTED`: Room 6 item/journal hints in later docs may reveal too much about where the Hexagonal Glass Piece is used; post-test fixes require no automatic hint.
- `UNKNOWN`: Whether duplicate `Pulse of Calm` scrolls can exist if both Room 4 and Room 6 drops are available.
